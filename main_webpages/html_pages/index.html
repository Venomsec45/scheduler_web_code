What Scheduler is all about

Scheduler is a schedule tracker that turns your tasks, classes and events into one clear weekly view. Instead of juggling sticky notes and three different apps, you add everything once and Scheduler keeps watch: it spots clashes, shows how full your days are and suggests better times when things pile up.
