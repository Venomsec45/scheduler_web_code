  1. 01
    September 2026

    Step 1: Supposed to be

    Our first 2 project poroposal ideas are the Pet shop website where users can buy a pet online and the budget tracker that tracks the budget and expenses of users.

  2. 02
    September 2026

    Step 2: Rejected

    Our 2 website project proposals got rejected. The first project proposal that got rejected due to financial, legal, safety, and livelihood limitations is the Pet shop website. The second website that was rejected is the budget tracker since making users connect their debit or credit card on the website requires a lot of agreement to National banks and extra information protection.

  3. 03
    October 2026

    Step 3: The idea

    Our team struggles to find an innovative project idea that will benefit the people until one day one of our team member suggested an idea that will change the lives of the people, the Schedule tracker, also known as the Scheduler. This website can help users organize their schedule and track progress.

  4. 04
    October 2026

    Step 4: Project proposal consultation

    Our team consults on our professor about our project proposal idea. Fortunately, our professor accepted our idea and we continued to plan about the frontend, backend, database, UI design, and role of the members. We began to identify risks and constraints that can affect our project development.

  5. 05
    November 2026

    Step 5: The development

    Our team members begin to share UI design ideas with each other. We started to create the frontend and the UI of our website. We started to build the backend of our website and connect it to our database

  6. 06
    November 2026

    Step 6: The final product

    Our team successfully created the functional website, Scheduler, also known as the schedule tracker. This website was still in production and hasn't released yet in the public.
